Answers get forgotten

Follow-ups slip

What ShortList does

Capture in one tap

Share a job from LinkedIn or Naukri on Android, or save it with the Chrome extension, description included. Spreadsheet import too.

Updates itself

Job emails and your applications list on job portals move statuses forward. Confirmation emails even create the application. Offers always wait for you; everything is undoable.

Follow up on time

One inbox for changes to review, follow-ups due and possible ghosting, with email and LinkedIn drafts you send yourself.

Prepare

A prep pack for each interview from the JD, your resume and your saved answers.

Ask your search

"How many rejections this month?" is answered exactly by the database. Other questions are answered with links to the records.

Yours

Self-host it free, bring your own AI key (or none), export everything, delete anytime. Salaries, contacts and emails are encrypted at rest.
